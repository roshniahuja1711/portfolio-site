Profile

Hello, I’m Roshni Ahuja!

I’m a recently graduated interior designer passionate about creating thoughtful, functional, and visually engaging spaces. Through my academic and internship experience, I have explored residential, commercial, and hospitality projects while gaining practical skills in design development, drawings, 3D visualization, material selection, and spatial planning.

I believe good design is about both how a space looks and how it feels and functions. As I begin my professional journey, I’m seeking an opportunity to learn, contribute, and grow alongside experienced designers while bringing creativity, curiosity, and dedication to every project.
